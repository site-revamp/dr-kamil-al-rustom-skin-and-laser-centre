About the centre

Dermatology and aesthetic care, brought together.

Dr. Kamil Al Rustom Skin & Laser Centre provides medical dermatology, aesthetic services and laser-based treatments for a range of skin and appearance-related concerns.

The clinic's approach brings specialist dermatological knowledge together with treatment options that can be discussed according to each patient's needs, medical history and consultation.
