# Dr. Kamil Al Rustom Skin & Laser Centre – Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Dr. Kamil Al Rustom Skin & Laser Centre is a medical dermatology and aesthetic clinic located in Al Bada'a, Dubai. Led by Consultant Dermatologist Dr. Kamil Al Rustom, the clinic provides medical dermatology, cosmetic treatments, laser procedures, and skin rejuvenation services.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating the clinic’s specialist expertise, making treatments easier to discover, and creating a clearer path for visitors to enquire or book an appointment.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Homepage Positioning
- Replaced the generic “We Are Skin Care Specialists” messaging with clearer positioning focused on consultant-led dermatology, aesthetic medicine, and laser treatments.
- Improved the presentation of the clinic’s specialist expertise and treatment focus.
- Made the homepage introduction more relevant to visitors seeking professional skin and laser care.

### 2. Improved Treatment Discovery and Organisation
- Organised the clinic’s services into clearer treatment categories.
- Improved the connection between common skin concerns and relevant treatment options.
- Made treatment information easier to navigate, reducing confusion caused by the previous presentation of multiple services and technologies.

### 3. Consistent Contact Information and Appointment Journey
- Addressed inconsistencies in the clinic’s published closing times by establishing a clearer contact information presentation.
- Improved the visibility and organisation of contact and appointment pathways.
- Made it easier for visitors to identify how to contact the clinic and take the next step toward an appointment.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual presentation:** Strengthened the professional medical and dermatology-focused appearance of the homepage.
- **Layout:** Improved content hierarchy and section organisation to give specialist services and key information clearer visibility.
- **User experience:** Simplified treatment discovery and made contact and appointment actions easier to find.
- **Consistency:** Improved the presentation of services, clinic information, and enquiry pathways.
- **Overall appearance:** Created a more structured homepage that communicates the clinic’s expertise and services more clearly.

## Project Objective

The redesign addresses three key issues in the original website: generic homepage positioning, unclear treatment discovery, and inconsistent contact information. The new homepage provides a clearer introduction to the clinic, a more organised service structure, and a more direct appointment enquiry journey.
